# Cookie Policy

This policy explains what cookies are, which ones this site uses and how you can control them. It complements the[Privacy Policy](https://battisti.com.br/privacy-policy/).

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files that a website stores in your browser to remember information from one page to the next.

## Which cookies this site uses

The site **uses no advertising cookies** and builds no browsing profiles. Fonts, scripts and images are served from this site itself, not from third parties. The only cookies are these:

- **Analytics: Google Analytics, only if you accept.** With your consent, Google Analytics stores the cookies `_ga` and `_ga_*` (for up to 2 years) to count visits and show which pages are viewed most, where visitors come from and how they move through the site. The data is used in aggregate. It is processed by Google, under [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

Your choice in the cookie notice is also kept in your browser (in `localStorage`, not in a cookie), so that the question is not asked again on every page.

## How to accept or decline

On your first visit, a notice asks whether you accept analytics cookies. Google Analytics is only loaded after you click **Accept**. To change your choice at any time, use(the same link is in the footer of every page). When you decline, the Google Analytics cookies are deleted.

## Links to other sites

Links to papers, DOIs, GitHub, YouTube, Google Scholar, Google Patents, Lattes and other sites take you away from this one. From there on, the privacy and cookie policies of each of those services apply.

## How to control cookies

You can view, block or delete cookies in your browser settings (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge and others). The site works the same with all cookies blocked.

## Changes to this policy

If other kinds of cookies come into use, this page and the cookie notice will be updated, and your consent will be asked before they are activated. The date of the last update is always at the top of the page.
