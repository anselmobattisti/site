# Anselmo Luiz Éden Battisti

I completed my PhD and Master’s in Computer Science at **Universidade Federal Fluminense (UFF)**, supervised by [Flávia Coimbra Delicato](https://lattes.cnpq.br/5386282151810710) and [Débora Christina Muchaluat-Saade](https://lattes.cnpq.br/2448703093928632). I also hold a Master’s in Strategic Business Management (PUCPR), a specialization in Java development, and a Bachelor’s in Computer Science (Unioeste).

Over two decades as a project manager and professor I have taken part in national and international projects in 5G, smart cities and digital platforms — work that produced scientific publications, patents registered in the United States, and solutions applied in the market. Today I teach at [**IMPA Tech**](https://impatech.edu.br/) and run [**Webgenium**](https://webgenium.com.br/), the company I co-founded in 2007.

## Research

[Publications, patents & awards →](https://battisti.com.br/research/)

I research the placement of Service Function Chains across the edge–cloud continuum — how to split a chain of virtual network functions over constrained edge nodes without breaking its latency budget. My PhD work, _SPEED_, solves it as a distributed decision instead of a central one.

A multimedia SFC across three tiers — the algorithm places only the two edge VNFs.

- SFC placement
- Edge–cloud continuum
- Virtual Network Functions
- 5G & network slicing
- Internet of Media Things
- VNF auto-scaling
- Domain-specific languages
