# Privacy Policy

This policy explains how this website handles the personal data of its visitors. It follows the Brazilian General Data Protection Law (LGPD, Law 13.709/2018).

## Who is responsible for your data

This is the personal and academic website of **Anselmo Luiz Éden Battisti**, who is the controller of the personal data processed here. For any matter concerning your personal data, write to[anselmo@battisti.com.br](mailto:anselmo@battisti.com.br).

## What data is collected

- **No forms, no accounts.** The site has no contact form, newsletter, comments or sign-up. It does not ask for your name, email or any other information.
- **Email.** If you write to the address on the contact page, your message and email address are used to reply to you.
- **Server data.** To deliver the pages, the web server processes your IP address, the date and time of the request and basic browser information, and may record them in technical logs.
- **Visit statistics, only with your consent.** If you accept analytics cookies, Google Analytics collects the pages viewed, where the visit came from, the type of device and the approximate city.

The site does not ask for sensitive data, such as health information, religion or political opinion.

## Why the data is used

- **To answer your message**, when you get in touch by email (legitimate interest, art. 7, IX, of the LGPD).
- **To keep the site working and secure**, protecting it against attacks and abuse (legitimate interest, art. 7, IX).
- **To understand how the site is used**, through aggregate visit statistics, only if you accept analytics cookies (consent, art. 7, I).
- **To comply with legal obligations**, when the law requires records to be kept (art. 7, II).

Personal data is never sold or rented.

## Who the data is shared with

- **The hosting provider** that runs the server where the site is published.
- **Google**, through Google Analytics, if you accept analytics cookies.
- **Public authorities**, when there is a court order or a legal obligation.

Some of these providers may process data outside Brazil. In those cases the transfer follows the rules of the LGPD (art. 33).

## How long the data is kept

- **Emails:** for as long as needed to reply and follow up on the conversation.
- **Technical server logs:** for up to 6 months.
- **Google Analytics cookies:** for up to 2 years, or until you decline them.

## Your rights

Under the LGPD (art. 18), you may ask at any time for:

- confirmation that your data is processed, and access to it;
- the correction of incomplete, inaccurate or outdated data;
- the anonymisation, blocking or deletion of unnecessary data, or of data processed against the law;
- the portability of your data;
- information about who your data has been shared with;
- the withdrawal of your consent, where the processing depends on it.

To exercise these rights, write to [anselmo@battisti.com.br](mailto:anselmo@battisti.com.br). You may also lodge a complaint with the Brazilian National Data Protection Authority (ANPD).

## Security

The site is served over an encrypted connection (HTTPS), and it stores no visitor database.

## Cookies

Analytics cookies (Google Analytics) are only used if you accept them in the cookie notice. Learn more and change your choice in the [Cookie Policy](https://battisti.com.br/cookie-policy/).

## Children and teenagers

The site is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated to reflect changes to the site or to the law. The date of the last update is always at the top of the page.
